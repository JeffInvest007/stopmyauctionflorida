You still have a window

The auction date is the whole game

In Florida, you keep the right to sell your property right up until the moment it's sold. Here's where the opportunity lives in each type of sale.

Case 1

Mortgage Foreclosure

  • 1Missed payments & default noticeThe lender starts the clock.
  • 2Lawsuit filed (Lis Pendens)Florida is a judicial-foreclosure state, so it goes through the court.
  • 3Final Judgment of ForeclosureThe judge sets a sale date, often 30 to 45 days out.
  • ★Your window to sell for cashPay off the debt before the sale and keep your remaining equity. A signed purchase agreement can also support a motion to cancel or reschedule the sale.
  • 4Foreclosure auctionAfter the gavel, the chance to protect your equity is gone.
The earlier you reach out, the more room there is to close cleanly before your sale date.
Case 2

Tax Deed Sale

  • 1Property taxes go unpaidThe county issues a tax certificate on the debt.
  • 2Certificate holder waitsAfter about two years, they can apply for a tax deed.
  • 3Clerk schedules the tax deed auctionA public sale date is set for the property.
  • ★Your window to redeem & sellYou can typically pay off (redeem) the amount owed any time before the sale. Selling for cash lets you clear it and capture your equity instead of losing the home over a tax bill.
  • 4Tax deed saleOnce sold, the property, and often most of its value, is lost.
Losing a home with real equity over unpaid taxes is one of the most avoidable outcomes in Florida real estate.

Frequently asked questions

How can I verify your company before I call? +
Please do, and take your time with it. We are an open book. You never pay us anything, at any point, for any reason. Closings happen at a licensed Florida title company, and every dollar moves through escrow, never hand to hand. Our company, Probate Assets Elite, LLC, is registered and active with the State of Florida, document number L24000159389, and you can verify it yourself on the state's official Sunbiz site. And we encourage you to have your own attorney look at anything before you sign it. We welcome that.
The home was my parent's and it is still in probate. Can you help? +
Yes. Probate is where we started, it is in our company's name. We work with personal representatives, executors, and heirs every week, and we coordinate with the probate attorney so the sale happens properly and on time. If you inherited a home that is behind on taxes or has a sale date coming, call us. This is exactly what we do.
Can you actually stop my auction? +
We can't promise to stop a sale. That depends on your lender, the court or clerk, and timing. What we can do is move quickly to close a cash purchase that pays off the debt before the auction. A signed purchase agreement with a real closing date can also support a motion to cancel or reschedule the sale.
How fast can you close? +
With clean title and cooperation from the lender or clerk, closings in as little as 7 to 14 days are realistic in Florida, and sometimes faster. The sooner you reach out before your sale date, the better.
Will I get any money out of it? +
If there's equity left after the payoff and standard closing costs, that money is yours. The whole point of you selling before the auction is to capture that equity instead of losing it at the sale.
What does this cost me? +
You pay no commissions or fees to us, and you sell as-is with no repairs. Standard closing costs are handled as agreed in your contract. There's no charge to get an offer.
What if it's a tax deed, not a mortgage? +
We handle both. With a tax deed sale you can typically redeem the property by paying what's owed any time before the auction. Selling for cash lets you clear the tax debt and keep your equity rather than losing the home over back taxes.
Do you buy the home, or just refer me to someone? +
Both can happen. On straightforward deals we buy directly. On more complex ones we bring in a trusted partner buyer we have worked with before, so the sale still closes on your timeline. Either way, you deal with us and we stay with you to the closing table.
Is this a loan, refinance, or "rescue" program? +
No. This is a straightforward sale of your property for cash. We're not a lender, law firm, or government program, and we don't offer loans or modifications.
